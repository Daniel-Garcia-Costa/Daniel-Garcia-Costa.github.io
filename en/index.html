Brasileirão Série A: analytical dashboard

A personal project built to answer a simple question: can you analyze the league without relying on a CSV that goes stale? (The project page is in Portuguese.)

A model that updates with the season

A Power BI dashboard covering three seasons (2024 to 2026). Data comes from the football-data.org API through Power Query M, and the model refreshes without manual work.

The core decision was to store two rows per match, one from each team's perspective. That turns summing points into a simple aggregation and makes home-advantage analysis almost free, without spreading USERELATIONSHIP across the measures.

What the data showed

  • Home teams win 47% of matches. Away teams win 26%. Home advantage is still the strongest variable in the league.
  • Grêmio takes 60% of available points at home and 12% away. It is the biggest home dependence of the season.
  • Flamengo leads both as a home and as an away team. Few leaders build their season on both sides.
  • Some clubs reached round 22 with a positive goal difference and ended negative. The final table shows the outcome; the round-by-round goal difference shows where the season went wrong.

What the project does not do

There are no expected goals (xG) in free sources for the Brazilian league, top scorer data is partial, and cards sit behind the API's paid plan. I documented these limits on the project page.
